# Cambio

Swap crypto by describing it in plain words, like *"swap 0.1 ETH to USDT on Tron"*, and get one quote for your exact
amount, with the network fee stated on its own line.

[cambio.one](https://cambio.one/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=profile&utm_campaign=profile-readme) · [X @cambioone](https://x.com/cambioone) · [Blog](https://cambio.one/blog?utm_source=github&utm_medium=profile&utm_campaign=profile-readme)

## Open data

We measure what instant crypto exchanges actually deliver, read from their own public websites, and publish every row.

- **[exchange-fee-data](https://github.com/cambio-one/exchange-fee-data)**: hourly checks of five exchanges at $200, $1,000
  and $5,000, each with the market price at the same moment. CSV, CC BY 4.0. Also on
  [Kaggle](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/cambioone/crypto-instant-exchange-real-costs).
- Written up: [What $200 and $1,000 of BTC → USDT on Tron really cost: 716 checks of five exchanges](https://cambio.one/blog/btc-to-usdt-real-cost-at-five-exchanges-september-2026?utm_source=github&utm_medium=profile&utm_campaign=profile-readme)

Found a mistake in the data? Open an issue on the repository. Corrections are published, including when they are ours.
